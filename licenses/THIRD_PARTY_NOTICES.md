# Third-party notices

## XRoboToolkit SDK

The Pico bridge imports the externally installed `xrobotoolkit_sdk` binding and
uses the separately installed XRoboToolkit PC Service. Neither binary nor its
Python package is redistributed by this repository. Obtain them from the vendor
release compatible with the installed PC Service and retain the vendor-provided
license terms with that installation.

The upstream XRoboToolkit teleoperation sample inspected during migration is
MIT-licensed, but its full source and assets are not included because they are
not required for the supported Franka/Pico bridge workflow.
